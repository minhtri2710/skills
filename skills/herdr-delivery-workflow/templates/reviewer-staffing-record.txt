INDEPENDENCE: <distinct-model-distinct-kind | distinct-model-same-kind | same-model-distinct-kind | same-model-same-kind> — the Reviewer is ALWAYS a distinct agent instance from every Engineer and the Lead; a same instance is never a review. In solo-Lead mode a different model from the Lead is REQUIRED and a different kind stays preferred, so only distinct-model-distinct-kind or distinct-model-same-kind is admissible
