Human-gate mailbox check (opt-in):
scripts/gate_row.py --ledger ~/.herdr/projects/<project-slug>/gates.md --check --mailbox ~/.herdr/projects/<project-slug>/supervisor-mailbox.md
# For an open push, merge, deploy, push-gate, merge-gate, or deploy-gate row, the mailbox must contain
# an exact-shape header:
# ## <lead> -> supervisor | <full-seconds ISO> | ATTENTION <text> | HEAD <sha>
# and <text> must name the row's gate id or exact full head SHA as a complete token.
# The header's HEAD field alone does not match. Without --mailbox, --check is unchanged.
